Subscription terms

Flights, costumes and Unlimited Flights subscriptions.

Flights and costumes

Unsubscribed players have five daily flight rights. A genuine start/restart consumes one. A voluntarily completed refill ad grants three rights once; cancellation, failure or unavailable ads grant nothing. Other optional rewards, including the wheel, keep their rules. Ordinary costumes cost 10,000 Zupin. Each of the nine ordinary costumes is also available as a one-time TRY 99.00 purchase in the Türkiye storefront. The purchase screen uses your storefront’s localized App Store price; the 10,000-Zupin alternative remains available. The game is free to download.

Unlimited Flights subscriptions

Saved Türkiye full-period prices are TRY 49.00 monthly, TRY 129.99 per three-month period and TRY 469.99 annually upfront. The purchase screen uses Apple’s actual localized full-period price for your storefront. Unless cancelled, the subscription renews automatically for the full period at the displayed price, charged to your Apple Account.

Every plan provides only Unlimited Flights while verified as entitled: no flight-right limit or refill-ad requirement. Other optional advertisements, including the wheel, remain available. Initial purchases, renewals, re-subscriptions and restore grant no Zupin. Earned Zupin and permanent costumes are not removed when a subscription ends.

Cancellation and offline access

Turning off renewal does not immediately end the valid period. Cancel in Apple subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel Apple billing.

Offline access lasts at most 24 hours from a successful explicit Apple verification or a new verified transaction, and never beyond verified membership/grace expiry. Reading the cache or reopening the app never extends it. After a phone reboot, a clock change or the deadline, connect to the internet and use Verify with Apple. Unavailable verification does not mean your subscription has ended. Offline permission grants no Zupin. An ongoing run continues; subsequent starts follow ordinary rights.

Apple terms

Apple’s Standard EULA remains in place. Sandbox/TestFlight transactions are tests; commercial App Store purchases charge real money.
