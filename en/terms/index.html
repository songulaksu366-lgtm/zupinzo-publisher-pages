Test use terms

There are no real-money or StoreKit purchases, or simulated payment transactions, in this test. Costume purchases with Zupin earned through genuine gameplay remain available.

What you can test

Test flight, obstacles, controls, fullscreen and safe areas, audio, local saves, characters, costume preview/equip, buying costumes with earned Zupin and menu navigation. Ordinary costumes cost 10,000 Zupin; Zupin cannot be bought with real money in this build.

The usual five daily flight rights apply. Ad-based refills and other ad rewards are unavailable; an unavailable ad grants nothing. Explicitly confirming the local test reset clears all test progress and returns to the usual starting state; it does not count as watching an ad.

Services outside this test

Sign in with Apple, server accounts, the online leaderboard, subscriptions, subscription Zupin bonuses, Unlimited Flights membership and server-account deletion/recovery are outside this build’s scope. An unavailable control is not evidence that its service works.

Registered subscription products and commercial prices are not purchase offers in this build. A future subscription test will be described separately; this build does not verify subscription transactions.

TestFlight and license

Apple’s test terms apply to TestFlight. The app’s Apple Standard License Agreement selection remains unchanged; this page is not a replacement custom EULA. Test builds may expire; permanent access and save migration are not promised.
