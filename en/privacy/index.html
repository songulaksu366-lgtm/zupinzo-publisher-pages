Privacy policy

This build runs as a guest. Game progress stays on the device; it does not connect to game-account or subscription servers.

Scope and publisher

The publisher is SUNA YILDIRIM. This invitation-only TestFlight gameplay test is for adult participants using their own iPhone and Apple Account before any public App Store release. ZUPINZO is intended for general players; this limited test does not establish a minimum age or Apple age rating for the commercial game.

Game data on your device

Language, audio and display settings, scores, local run history, earned Zupin, costumes, achievements, missions and flight rights are stored in a separate local test save. This build does not send those saves to the publisher’s server or submit scores to a global leaderboard. Game accounts, Sign in with Apple, subscription purchases and subscription restore are unavailable.

This build has no advertising SDK, live advertising or game-to-server analytics service. This description does not cover data processing in future advertising-enabled or online builds.

Apple, support and this website

Apple automatically collects TestFlight crash and usage data and shares it with the developer. Your name/email associated with your TestFlight identity and feedback you submit may also be shared with the publisher. Apple processes this information under its own TestFlight privacy terms. Your TestFlight invitation identity is not a game account.

If you email support, your address, message and attachments reach SUNA YILDIRIM through Gmail/Google. Do not send passwords, verification codes or payment information. The publisher manages correspondence manually and removes closed support cases after 30 days; this does not mean Google’s own copies disappear at the same time.

This static website uses no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages may process IP/connection and technical access information under its own privacy terms.

Removing saves and practical limits

Confirming “Reset local test progress” in Settings clears only this test’s game progress and settings. It leaves other profiles untouched. There is no server game account to delete in this build. The button does not delete Apple/TestFlight data or support emails.

Test saves are not automatically transferred to future account-enabled or commercial builds. Recovery after reinstalling, deleting the app or losing TestFlight access is not guaranteed. Operating-system and provider backups may be separate. Send support-data requests to the address below.
