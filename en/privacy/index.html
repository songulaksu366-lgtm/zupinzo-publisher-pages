Privacy policy

Data for local progress, Apple services, advertising and support.

Publisher and scope

The publisher is SUNA YILDIRIM. The game targets general players; this creates no age restriction or privacy exemption. Earlier test versions have separate disclosures.

Local progress

No publisher account is required to play or use StoreKit. Language, audio, scores, earned Zupin, costumes, equipment, missions and flight rights stay on this device. Cross-device synchronization and recovery after deleting the app are not promised for earned Zupin or local progress. Previous guest/account saves are not merged or erased. Test data is not imported into commercial saves. Verified Apple access information is held in the device’s protected Keychain; removing the app is not guaranteed to clear every Keychain record. The publisher cannot remotely erase device saves.

Apple services

Apple handles payments. The app uses verified transactions, products, environments and membership information on the device. The publisher does not receive card details. StoreKit purchase identity, Sign in with Apple identity and Game Center identity are distinct.

Game Center and online rankings are not enabled in this version; scores are not sent to a publisher server. Game statistics stay on the device; the app’s own analytics module has no network provider.

Advertisements

Google Mobile Ads and UMP may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, performance and diagnostics. Reduced personalization, test ads or no ATT prompt do not imply no data processing or tracking. This version disables ad personalization and publisher first-party ID and uses Google UMP for regional consent. Privacy options can be reopened in settings when required. Missing permission or an unavailable ad does not earn a completed-ad reward. The app does not request tracking permission. Data processing for ad measurement, fraud prevention and operation of the service may still occur. Access Privacy options from Settings; Google’s consent form opens when required.

Support and providers

SUNA YILDIRIM handles support through Gmail/Google, using your email address, message and attachments. This static site has no advertising/analytics script; GitHub Pages may process technical connection data under its own terms.

The publisher manually deletes correspondence for a closed support case 30 days after closure. An open case is kept for support; this does not guarantee immediate secure erasure from provider backups. Apple, Google and GitHub may process data under their own terms, including on systems outside your country. The publisher cannot erase all provider copies simultaneously. Separate test rules apply to earlier private-test data.
